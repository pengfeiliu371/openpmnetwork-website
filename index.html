Research direction

More than a low-cost concentration reading

Optical PM sensors respond to particle size, humidity, composition, optical properties, and instrument state. Open PM Network preserves the inputs needed to study those influences rather than hiding them behind a single number.

Current boundary

Low-cost measurements are not substitutes for regulatory or research-grade reference methods. Corrections must be validated against clearly defined, collocated reference measurements.

01

Complete output retention

Keep manufacturer-accessible measurements, status fields, and unknown future schema fields for reprocessing.

02

Physics-informed calibration

Evaluate how size, humidity, hygroscopicity, and optical properties influence sensor response.

03

Uncertainty-aware QA/QC

Pair corrected values with quality flags, calibration domain checks, and sensor-health evidence.

04

Event-responsive sampling

Explore plume detection and autonomous control of filter sampling for subsequent analysis.

Early field work

Encouraging consistency, carefully described

The first outdoor SPS30 deployment achieved 97.3% coverage of eligible hourly bins and followed major variations seen across nearby sensor networks. Because the instruments were not collocated, this is a field consistency check—not a calibration or accuracy validation.

Community and classrooms

Simple participation without giving up data governance

The first community workflow is designed around three actions: add a sensor, view or download data, and choose whether to make a deployment public.

⌁

Teacher-managed pilots

School staff manage devices. Students can explore approved classroom data without creating personal accounts.

◎

Location privacy

Private and public coordinates are separate. Owners choose the public location precision and preview it before approval.

◇

Responsible openness

Public data includes attribution, units, quality context, and a clear distinction between access approval and scientific certification.

Roadmap

Start small.
Build evidence.
Open carefully.

  1. Phase 1
    Research team pilot

    Validate complete SPS30 data capture, offline recovery, private uploads, and deployment history with 2–20 nodes.

  2. Phase 2
    K–12 pilot

    Add teacher-managed deployment, simple classroom views, downloads, and school-level attribution.

  3. Phase 3
    Public community network

    Expand self-service registration, public APIs, integrations, and governance when real demand appears.
